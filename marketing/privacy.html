Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

The short version

Bench Buddy stores your team roster and game settings so it can build fair substitution rotations and keep working the next time you open it. That data lives in Google Firebase, the infrastructure Bench Buddy runs on. It is never sold, and it is never used for advertising — there is no advertising in Bench Buddy at all.

What Bench Buddy collects

Two kinds of information:

  • What you type in: your team's name, the names and shirt numbers of the players you add, and the game settings you choose (squad size, game length, sub interval, goalkeeper rules).
  • Your account: if you sign in (for example with Google), Bench Buddy receives the basic profile information that sign-in method provides — typically your name and email address — so your teams are saved to your account rather than lost when you close the app.

Bench Buddy does not ask for a child's date of birth, address, photo, or any contact details for a player. A player's entry in a roster is a first name and, optionally, a shirt number — entered by the coach running the team, not by the child.

Why it's collected

Solely to run the app: building a rotation, keeping score of playing time and goalkeeper turns during a match, and making sure your team is there the next time you sign in. Nothing you enter is used to build advertising profiles, and Bench Buddy has no ad network of any kind integrated into it.

Where it's stored

In Firebase (Google Cloud), using the security and access-control tools Firebase provides. Your teams are only readable by your own signed-in account — nobody else's rotations are visible to you, and yours aren't visible to them.

Children's information

Bench Buddy is a tool for coaches and team organisers, not an app aimed at children, and it collects no information directly from a child. Rosters are entered and managed by the adult running the team. If you're a parent or club with a concern about a specific team's data, contact us (below) and we'll help.

Third parties

Firebase (Google Cloud) is the only third party involved, and it acts purely as infrastructure — the same role a filing cabinet plays for paper records. Bench Buddy does not share, sell, or rent your data to anyone else, for any reason.

Your choices

You can remove players from a roster or delete a team at any time inside the app. If you'd like your account and every team on it permanently deleted, contact us and we'll action it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, the date at the top of this page will change with it.

Contact

Questions about your data or this policy: hello@benchbuddysports.com.
