Why Coaches and Parents love it

Five fewer things to hold in your head.

Fair from the first whistle

Playing time is shared evenly before the match starts, so every kid gets the minutes they need to develop.

Handles the unexpected

Injuries, late arrivals, a change of shape at half time. The rotation re-plans itself and stays fair.

Goalkeeper rotations made easy

Set who goes in goal and when. Everyone gets a turn between the posts without you tracking it.

Never caught out by a sub

An early nudge before each change, so the next player is warmed up and ready on the touchline.

A record of the whole season

Minutes, positions and goalkeeping turns add up week to week, so you can show any parent where their child stands.

Interaction & build notes

Video

The sixteen clips currently stream straight from the repo (raw.githubusercontent.com/…/docs/Marketing Videos/). That is a staging arrangement only — it depends on the repo staying public and serves 720p with no range support, so production must host them on a CDN with a poster JPG per clip for first paint.

Autoplay constraint, do not skip: muted and loop must be set as DOM properties in script (v.muted = true; v.loop = true), not as markup attributes. Rendered through React the bare attributes arrive as false, autoplay is refused with NotAllowedError, and every slot sits on a frozen first frame. A failed video also paints opaque over its own placeholder, so hide it on error and reveal it on loadeddata.

Story, three acts

Three acts, not a comparison. Two earlier versions put Dave and Emma next to each other — first as a horizontal snap rail, then as paired columns — and both asked the reader to hold two Saturdays at once before they were sold on either. The rebuild tells the familiar one first (Act one, seven beats), turns on a single full-bleed moment (The turn — Dave is asked to referee), then answers it beat for beat (Act two, five rows plus a full-width closing frame).

Both acts use the same row: a 4/3 frame at flex: 1 1 400px beside a 1 1 288px text column, wrapping to stacked around 720px — no breakpoints, the content decides. An earlier Act one used small square thumbnails to move faster, but the change of form read as a different kind of content rather than the first half of one story. Only the accent separates the acts: Act two’s frames carry a yellow top edge and yellow numerals, Act one’s are plain cream.

All fourteen story frames now point at real clips (Act one 7, the turn 1, Act two 6). Four clips in the repo are unused: Dave’s Scene 1 (superseded by the lower-quality cut in Act one 01) and Scene 6, plus Parent Emma — Sideline Parent asking whos next and Coaches talking after the game.

Playback is gated on visibility: one IntersectionObserver (rootMargin: 25% 0px) plays a clip as it approaches and pauses once it leaves. Story clips are preload="none" — with fourteen videos across the three acts, metadata preload alone would cost fourteen requests on load. Keep the observer when moving to a CDN.

Motion

One effect only: sections fade up 18px over 700 ms, cubic-bezier(.22,.7,.25,1), once per element. An IntersectionObserver fires at threshold 0.01 with rootMargin: 0 0 -8% 0, resolving its root to the real scrolling ancestor rather than assuming the viewport. A 1200 ms failsafe then reveals anything still hidden — that is what keeps the page legible if the observer never fires (host-owned scroll container, print, odd embed), and it is why revealed elements are flagged so a re-render cannot hide them again. No parallax, no counters, no scroll-jacking. Fully suppressed under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.

Responsive

Fluid rather than breakpoint-driven: type uses clamp(), grids use auto-fit / minmax, so 3-up collapses to 2-up then 1-up on its own. Hero is 100svh so mobile browser chrome does not crop the CTA. Verified 360–1920 px.

Accessibility

Every text colour is measured against its resolved background, not assumed: body #4C6157 on #F4F1E9 is 6.1:1, eyebrow #1F5637 on the darker cream #EAE5D8 is 6.0:1, footer links and the footer note at 66% cream on #14251D are 7.0:1, #14251D on #F5B93B is 9.1:1, notes body #485A4E on #DED8C8 is 5.2:1. The app green #2E7D53 is not used for small text anywhere — it only reaches 4.0:1 on #EAE5D8 — so it is confined to graphic marks. Hero text sits on a 50% scrim so contrast holds over any frame. Every tap target clears 44 px. All sixteen videos are decorative and aria-hidden, as are the benefit marks; phone screenshots carry real alt text.

Type & colour

Schibsted Grotesk 700 for headings (tight −3% tracking), Nunito 500 for body, Baloo 2 800 for the wordmark only — the one thread back to the app. Two backgrounds all the way down: bone #F4F1E9 and deep green #14251D, with #EAE5D8 as the single step between. Yellow #F5B93B is reserved for actions and never used as a surface.
